Questions

Does it work for one store?

Yes. Starter is free forever for one location with up to 15 staff.

Can it check award and break rules?

Business plans check break and overtime rules you configure, and flag shifts that break them before you publish.

How does AI demand forecasting work?

It uses your past sales and foot traffic to suggest how many people you need each hour. You stay in control of the final roster.

Is my data safe?

In this demo nothing leaves your browser. A real product would publish its security and privacy practices here.
